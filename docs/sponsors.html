Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about sponsoring, payments, and billing details.

📍 Can we remove the address requirement during checkout?

Yes, the physical street address is NOT required! Because AgentShield and RitualDev tools are purely digital open-source software, physical shipping addresses are disabled.

What is collected and why? Only your Country and Zip / Postal Code are requested. This is legally required by international tax regulations because Dodo Payments operates as a registered Merchant of Record (MoR). It ensures correct VAT / GST calculation (or 0% tax exemption) and enables fraud protection (AVS). You do not need to enter your house number or street name.

💳 What payment methods are accepted? Can I pay with UPI in India?

Yes! Indian supporters can pay effortlessly using UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), NetBanking, and RuPay cards. Worldwide supporters can pay using Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex), Apple Pay, and Google Pay in over 135 currencies.

📄 Can enterprises get a tax-compliant business invoice?

Absolutely. During checkout, enter your company name and GSTIN / VAT number. Dodo Payments will automatically generate a legally compliant business PDF invoice that you can immediately file for expense reimbursement or tax deduction.

🏆 How quickly will my name and comment appear on the Leaderboard?

Immediately upon clicking "Proceed to Checkout", your entry is cached and displayed in the leaderboard and community feed. Once the checkout completes, our webhook confirms your permanent verified backer status.
